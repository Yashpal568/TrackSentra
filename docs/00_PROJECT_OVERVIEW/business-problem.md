# Business Problem

## Current situation
A guard may be instructed to patrol a factory perimeter, warehouse, campus, or industrial facility at scheduled intervals. Management needs evidence that the route was actually completed.

## Failure modes
- Patrol skipped
- Checkpoint skipped
- Patrol completed late
- Wrong sequence
- QR photographed and scanned remotely
- Guard identity mismatch
- Poor manual records
- No centralized reporting

## Product response
The platform combines guard authentication, checkpoint identity, patrol sequence, timestamp, GPS proximity, device/session information, and audit events.
