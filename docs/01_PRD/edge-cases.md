# Edge Cases

- QR scanned twice rapidly
- QR scanned out of order
- QR scanned from outside GPS radius
- GPS unavailable
- GPS accuracy too poor
- location permission denied
- browser offline
- network drops after scan
- request retried
- device clock incorrect
- patrol expires while guard is scanning
- guard is deactivated during patrol
- checkpoint deactivated during patrol
- shift crosses midnight
- duplicate QR identity
- QR replaced physically
- supervisor edits route while patrol is active
- company/site disabled
